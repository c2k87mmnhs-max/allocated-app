Features

Every tool you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built around the envelope budgeting method — the same proven approach that YNAB made popular, refined into a cleaner, faster macOS app.

✉️

Envelope Budgeting

Give every dollar a job before the month starts. Move money between envelopes as life happens — no guilt, just clarity.

🏦

Savings Goal Folders

Track progress toward Emergency Fund, Travel, a new laptop — anything. Visual progress bars and monthly contribution tracking built in.

📥

CSV Bank Import

Import transactions from Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Capital One, and Citi. Smart auto-categorization with custom rules.

📅

Monthly Workflow

A guided wizard walks you through starting each month — log your paycheck, assign your dollars, review your goals. Takes 5 minutes.

📊

End of Month Report

Review your spending, cover any overspending, sweep surplus to savings. A complete month-close ritual built right into the app.

📈

Net Worth Tracking

Cash, savings, investments, CDs, and liabilities in one place. Auto-updated monthly chart so you can watch your net worth grow.

🔒

Your Data, Your Machine

Everything is stored in a single JSON file on your Mac. Automatic rolling backups. No cloud, no account, no breach risk.

🎓

Built for Beginners

A full setup wizard, newbie mode with inline tips, and a comprehensive help section mean you don't need to read a manual.

📤

Spending Reports Export

Export a clean CSV of your spending by category across any date range. Share with a financial advisor or just keep for your records.
